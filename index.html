Music Maker

Paste a tune idea as JSON, drop a file, or sketch directly on the grid. The app turns it into a playable pattern with melody and drums.

Accepted JSON

{
  "title": "Neon Steps",
  "tempo": 120,
  "steps": 8,
  "notes": [{ "step": 0, "note": "G4", "length": 2 }],
  "drums": [{ "step": 2, "lane": "kick" }]
}
